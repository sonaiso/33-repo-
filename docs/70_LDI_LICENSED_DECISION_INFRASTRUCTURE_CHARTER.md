## 1) Honest Product Definition

`LDI` is a narrow decision-governance product pattern:

- deterministic gating before authority,
- explicit proposer/decider separation,
- residual-first failure handling,
- traceable decision lineage.

It is **not** AGI and is not a claim of general intelligence.
